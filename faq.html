Frequently asked questions

How quoting, scheduling and pricing work at Maxcel Services. If your question isn’t here, call (703) 629-6602 — we’d rather answer it directly.

Quotes & pricing

Before you book

How do I get a quote?

Call (703) 629-6602 and describe the job, or send the quote form. The call is faster, because we can ask the questions that affect the price while you’re on the line.

What will the work cost?

It depends on the job, which is why we quote rather than publish a flat price list. You’ll have the figure before work starts, and the invoice will match it unless you ask us to change the scope along the way.

Am I committed once I ask for a quote?

No. Asking for a quote costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. Work only goes ahead once you’ve agreed to the scope and the price.

What information should I have ready?

Where the work is, what you want done, whether there’s a deadline, and anything that’s already been tried or quoted. None of it is essential — we’ll ask what we need — but it makes the first call much more useful.

What happens if something unexpected comes up mid-job?

We stop and tell you. Anything that changes the scope or the cost gets raised with you before it’s actioned, so you’re never presented with it for the first time on the invoice.

Scheduling & contact

Getting hold of us

How quickly will I hear back?

A phone call is always the fastest route — that’s why the number sits at the top of every page. Quote forms and emails are answered as soon as we can get to them. If your job is time-sensitive, please call (703) 629-6602 rather than waiting on a reply.

Do you cover my area?

Call (703) 629-6602 with your location and we’ll confirm straight away whether we can take the job on. If we can’t, we’ll say so on that first call instead of leaving you waiting.

Can I reach a person, not a menu?

That’s the idea. One number, one point of contact for your job, and no re-explaining it to someone new every time you ring.

Do you take email enquiries?

Yes — ken@maxcelservices.com. Anything you send through the forms on this site arrives at the same place.

What happens to the details I send through the form?

They come to us and are used to reply to your enquiry. We don’t sell your details, and we don’t add you to a mailing list you didn’t ask for.

Do you publish customer reviews?

Only real ones. We don’t write testimonials for ourselves — see the reviews page for how to leave feedback or ask us for references.
