Common questions

Answers before you pick up the phone

How do I get a quote?

Two ways: call (703) 629-6602 and describe the job, or fill in the quote form. Either way you’ll get the scope and price confirmed before anything is booked.

What will the work cost?

It depends on the job, which is why we quote rather than publish a flat price list. What we can promise is that you’ll have the figure before we start, and the invoice will match it unless you ask us to change the scope.

How quickly will I hear back?

A phone call is always the fastest route — that’s why the number is at the top of every page. Quote forms and emails are answered as soon as we can get to them; if your job is time-sensitive, please call instead of waiting on a reply.

Do you cover my area?

Call (703) 629-6602 with your location and we’ll confirm straight away whether we can take the job on. If we can’t, we’ll tell you on that first call.

Am I committed once I ask for a quote?

No. Asking for a quote costs you nothing and commits you to nothing. The work only goes ahead once you’ve agreed to the scope and the price.
