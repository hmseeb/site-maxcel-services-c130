What working with Maxcel Services looks like

One point of contact, a quote you agree to before anything starts, and a booked date you can plan around. Call (703) 629-6602 to talk about your job.

Quotes & estimates

Know the cost before you commit

The quote is where most problems get avoided. We ask the questions that actually affect the price — access, condition, materials, timing — so the figure you get is the figure you pay, not an opening bid that drifts upward once work has begun.

  • A clear statement of what’s included and what isn’t
  • Anything that could change the price flagged up front
  • Time to think it over — asking for a quote commits you to nothing
  • Questions answered by someone who knows the job, not a script

What to have ready when you call

None of this is essential — but it gets you a sharper answer on the first call.

  • Where the work is and how easy it is to get to
  • What you want done, in plain terms
  • Whether there’s a deadline or a date to work around
  • Anything that’s already been attempted or quoted

Scheduling

A date that holds — and a call if it can’t

Once you’ve agreed the quote, we confirm the date and the arrival window with you directly. If something genuinely has to move, you hear it from us as soon as we know, not after you’ve taken a morning off.

You don’t have to chase us for an update, and you don’t have to re-explain your job to a different person every time you ring.

Before, during, after

Before: scope and price confirmed, date agreed.

During: we work to the agreed scope; anything unexpected is raised with you before it’s actioned.

After: the site is tidied, and the invoice matches the quote.

Other ways we work

Repeat visits and multi-stage projects

Repeat & ongoing work

Stop briefing a stranger every time

For work you need more than once, you keep the same point of contact. Your property, your access arrangements and your preferences stay on file with us, so each visit starts where the last one finished.

Larger projects

A big job you can actually follow

Multi-visit work is broken into stages with the cost and the sequence set out up front, so you always know what’s happening next and what it’s for.

Not sure if it’s a fit?

Ask on the first call and get a straight yes or no

We’d rather tell you in two minutes that your job isn’t one for us than leave you waiting a week for a quote that never arrives. Call (703) 629-6602, describe what you need, and you’ll get a direct answer — including, where we can, a pointer in the right direction.
