Reviews and references

Real feedback from real customers, or nothing at all. Here’s how to check us out before you book — and how to tell us how we did.

Our position

No invented testimonials on this website

Plenty of service websites open with five-star quotes from people who were never customers. We’re not doing that. There are no made-up names, no stock photos of strangers and no star rating on this page, because we won’t publish proof we can’t stand behind.

As verified customer feedback comes in through the form below, it gets published here — in the customer’s own words.

Ask for references

When we quote your job, ask us about comparable work we’ve done. That’s a far better check than a quote on a website.

Ask us anything on the call

How we price, what’s included, how we handle problems mid-job — all fair questions, and you’ll get direct answers on the phone.

Already a customer?

Use the form below. Positive or critical, we’d rather hear it than not — it’s how the service gets better.
